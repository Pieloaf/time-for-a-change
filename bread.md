## Intro to Sourdough Workshop
##### 📅 Sunday, 18th September, 6-8pm
##### 📍 Night Time Coop @ My Goodness Café, 24 Sullivan's Quay, Cork, T12 X867
##### €15 · 7 spaces only · 17+
---

Do you want to get hands-on with the process of baking sourdough bread at home, on a schedule that works for you?

**What we'll cover:**
- The fundamentals: equipment, ingredients, and baker's percentages
- The magic: timing, fermentation, and maintaining your starter
- The process: get hands-on with shaping and baking!

**What to bring:**
- A small jar or vessel (to bring home your own starter)
- A banneton basket OR a mixing bowl lined with a clean tea towel (to transport your dough home for baking)
